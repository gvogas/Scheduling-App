1. Who this policy covers

The App is used by two kinds of people:

  • Account holders — the business owner/administrator and the employees they invite, who sign in to use the App.
  • Clients — the customers of a business that uses the App, whose contact and appointment details are entered into the App by the account holders. Clients do not sign in to the App themselves.

A business that uses the App to manage its own customers is responsible for the client information it enters and decides why and how that information is collected and used. In that context, we act as a service provider (processor) that stores and processes the information on the business’s behalf and under this policy. If you are a client of such a business and have questions about your information, please contact that business directly; you may also contact us using the details below.

2.2 Device features and permissions

These are used only when you choose to use the related feature:

  • Camera — to take appointment photos.
  • Photo library — to attach existing images, and, if you tap “Save to Photos” on an appointment photo, to add that photo to your own photo library. Saving asks only for add-only access. You can also share a photo out of the App using the standard iOS share sheet; where it goes is then up to you.
  • Contacts — to save a client you choose into your phone’s contacts, and to keep that one contact up to date if you later edit the client in the App. To do that, the App reads back only the contact it created for that client. It does not read, collect, or upload the rest of your contact list, and nothing from your contacts is sent to us.
  • Location (only while the App is open) — if you are an employee or administrator and you grant location access, the App uses your device’s location to power “time to leave” reminders that tell you when to depart for your next appointment. The App collects location only while it is open on your screen; it does not track your location in the background, and it only ever asks for “While Using the App” access. See Section 2.4 for exactly what is collected, how it is used, and how to turn it off. This feature is optional: if you deny location access, the reminders fall back to a simpler estimate and everything else keeps working.
  • Face ID / biometrics — to unlock the optional in-app lock you can enable in Settings. Biometric data is processed entirely by iOS on your device and never leaves it; we do not receive or store it.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Diagnostics and crash data. We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and limited technical diagnostics (for example, device model, operating system version, and the technical state of the App at the time of a crash) so that we can identify and fix problems.
  • Usage analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the App’s features are used — for example, which screens are opened, and when an appointment is created, a search is run, or a setting is changed. These records describe only the kind of action and its general shape (such as where in the App it happened, whether a job has photos, or an approximate count); they never include names, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, notes, photos, or what you type into a search. Alongside them we record your role in the App (administrator or employee), the App’s language, and standard technical information (an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system and App version, and an approximate region that Google derives from your internet connection). We do not give Google Analytics your account ID, and this information is not used for advertising or to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • App integrity signals. To protect the service, the App uses Apple App Attest and Firebase App Check to verify that requests come from a genuine, untampered copy of the App. This process uses a device-generated attestation and does not identify you personally.
  • Address lookups. When you type an address, the partial text is sent, through our secure backend, to Google Maps Platform (Places API) to return address suggestions.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, the App registers a device push token (provided by Apple Push Notification service through Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you job-related notifications — such as a new or changed appointment, an upcoming-job reminder, a daily summary, or a “job finished?” prompt. The token is a device identifier used to deliver messages to your device; it is not used to track you and can be revoked at any time by turning off notifications for the App in your device Settings.
  • Live Activity token. If you use the optional Lock Screen “live job card” (iOS Live Activities), the App registers a second, short-lived push token for each card so it can keep the card up to date. These tokens are stored in your own record, are deleted when the card ends, when you sign out, or when your account is deleted or disabled, and are used only to update that card. You can turn the feature off in Settings → Notifications. See Section 2.5 for what the card displays.

2.4 Location for “time to leave” reminders

To help employees and administrators arrive at appointments on time, the App can calculate how long it will take to drive to your next job and remind you when to leave. This feature works only if you allow location access, and you can turn it off at any time (see below).

  • What is collected. Your device’s approximate geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) and the time of the reading. Location is reported only while the App is open on your screen. The moment you switch to another app, return to the Home Screen, or lock your device, your device stops reporting location to the App, and it resumes only when you open the App again. The App does not use the iOS location background mode and does not ask for “Always” access.
  • How often. To limit data collection and save battery, a new location is recorded only after you have moved a meaningful distance (about 250 metres) or roughly every couple of minutes, with an occasional check-in so a stationary location stays current.
  • How it is used. Your most recent location is used as the starting point to estimate live, traffic-aware drive time to your next appointment (via Google Maps Platform, through our secure backend) so the App can send the reminder at the right moment. If you are an employee or administrator, administrators can also view your single most recent location on a live staff map in the App while location sharing is active, labelled with how long ago it was recorded, however old that reading is. It is not used to monitor your movements over time or build a location history — only the latest reading is ever available, never a trail of where you have been.
  • Where it is stored and who can see it. Only your single most recent location is stored, in your own record in our database. It is readable by you, by the backend that calculates your reminder, and by administrators, who can view each active staff member’s most recent location — but not a history or trail — on a live map in the App while location sharing is active. It is not shown to other, non-administrator employees. It is overwritten each time a newer reading is taken. When an administrator views the live map, your coordinates may also be sent, through our secure backend, to Google’s Geocoding service so the App can display a street address instead of raw coordinates.
  • How to turn it off. Deny or revoke location permission for the App in your device Settings. Because the App only reads your location while it is open, simply leaving the App also stops collection. Your stored location is deleted when you sign out or delete your account, and it is automatically removed from our systems if your account is deleted or disabled by an administrator.

2.5 Home-screen widget, Siri, and the Lock Screen job card

On iOS, the App can show your own upcoming jobs outside the App itself. These features show you information you already have access to in the App; they are optional and each can be turned off.

  • Home-screen widget and Siri. The App writes a small snapshot of your own upcoming jobs — such as the time, the client or job name, and the address — into a private storage area shared between the App and its widget on your device, so the widget can display it and Siri can answer questions like “what’s my next job?”. The snapshot itself is a file on your device: it is not uploaded to us, and neither we nor Apple can read it. It is removed when you sign out or delete the App. Because the widget has to stay current while the App is closed, the job details it shows are refreshed by a silent push message, which — like any notification — reaches your device through Apple’s push notification service. Remove the widget, or turn off Siri access for the App in your device Settings, to stop using these features.
  • Lock Screen job card (Live Activities). If enabled, a card on your Lock Screen shows your current or next job — the client or job name, the address, and the time. Because the card is updated while the App is closed, this information is delivered to your device through Apple’s push notification service, in the same way as a notification. Note that the card is visible on your Lock Screen without unlocking your device; turn the feature off in Settings → Notifications if that is not appropriate for your work.

The App does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The only location the App collects is for the optional “time to leave” reminder feature described in Section 2.4, and only if you grant location access.

3. How and why we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create, secure, and operate accounts, and authenticate users;
  • provide the core scheduling features — appointments, clients, and employees — and control which appointments each user can see;
  • store and display the records and photos you create;
  • send job-related push notifications about your appointments (if you allow notifications) — including a notice to you if an administrator changes the email address you sign in with — and, on iOS, show your own upcoming jobs in the optional home-screen widget, through Siri, and on the optional Lock Screen job card (Section 2.5);
  • if an administrator connects the optional Wave accounting integration, keep client records in step between the App and the business’s Wave account (Section 4);
  • provide address autocomplete when you enter an address;
  • if you enable location access, estimate traffic-aware drive time to your next appointment and send you a “time to leave” reminder (Section 2.4);
  • understand, in aggregate, how the App’s features are used so that we can improve them (Section 2.3);
  • diagnose crashes, maintain security, and improve reliability; and
  • comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Legal basis. We process personal information on the basis of your consent, to perform the service you request, for our legitimate business interest in operating and securing the App, and to meet legal obligations. For client information entered by a business, the business is responsible for having the appropriate basis to collect and use that information. You may withdraw consent as described in Section 8; withdrawing consent may limit features that depend on the information.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account information is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, your account record, profile, and emergency contact are removed (see Section 8). An administrator can also disable an employee’s account, which immediately ends that person’s access and deletes their stored location, push tokens, and Lock Screen job cards; the profile record itself is kept, because past appointments refer to it. Once you have completed setup, an administrator can disable your account but cannot delete it — only you can, from within the App. Before you have completed setup, while the account is still pending first sign-in, an administrator can remove it outright.
  • Client records created within a business remain part of that business’s records for as long as the business keeps them. A client that is no longer active is normally archived rather than deleted: an archived client is hidden from the main list but is retained, and stays searchable, so that the appointment history that refers to them is not broken. A client can be deleted outright only while they have no appointment history at all.
  • Appointment and photo records are deleted when the business deletes them, and completed or cancelled appointments — together with any photos attached to them — are in any case deleted automatically two years after the appointment date. Appointments that are not yet complete are never deleted automatically, however old they are.
  • Crash diagnostics are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period in accordance with Google’s policies.
  • Usage analytics are retained by Google Analytics for Firebase for a limited period in accordance with Google’s policies.
  • Push notification tokens are removed when you sign out on that device, when you delete your account, or when an administrator disables or deletes your account. Turning notifications off in your device Settings stops the notifications from being shown, but does not by itself delete the stored token — sign out on the device if you want it removed. Lock Screen job card tokens are removed when the card ends, when you turn the feature off, or when you sign out.
  • Location for reminders is not kept as a history: only your single most recent reading is stored, and each new reading overwrites the last. It is deleted when you sign out, delete your account, or have your account deleted or disabled by an administrator. Revoking location permission stops any further readings being taken, but does not by itself delete the reading already stored: until one of the events above occurs, that last reading remains. Administrators can see it on the live map, shown with how long ago it was recorded, for as long as it is stored and location sharing is turned on. Sign out if you want it removed straight away.

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. How we protect information

  • Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by our cloud providers.
  • Access is restricted by security rules enforced on the backend: an employee can see only the appointments assigned to them, an employee whose account has not yet been set up can see nothing at all, administrative functions require an administrator account, and an emergency contact can be read only by the person it belongs to and by an administrator.
  • Requests to our database and backend functions require Apple App Attest / Firebase App Check verification, which blocks requests that do not come from a genuine, untampered copy of the App.
  • The optional in-app lock (Face ID / passcode) can be enabled in Settings to require your biometrics before the App can be viewed.
  • Sensitive server-side keys are stored in a managed secrets service and are never included in the App.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect personal information against loss, theft, and unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to obtain information about how your information is handled. Residents of Quebec and elsewhere in Canada may have additional rights under the Private Sector Act (Law 25) and PIPEDA.

  • Delete your account. You can delete your account and associated profile at any time from within the App (Settings → Account → Delete account). This is available in the App and does not require contacting us.
  • Access and correction. You can view and edit much of your information directly in the App — including your emergency contact, in Settings → My details, which you can clear at any time. The rest of an employee’s profile (name, phone number, job title, working hours, email address, and role) is maintained by the administrator of your business; ask them to correct it, or contact us using the details below.
  • Deletion and withdrawal of consent. You may ask us to delete your personal information or withdraw your consent to certain processing. Some information may be retained where required by law.
  • Device permissions. You can grant or revoke camera, photo, contacts, location, and Face ID permissions at any time in your device’s Settings; features that rely on a permission will be limited if it is revoked. Turning off location access stops the App taking any further readings, which ends the “time to leave” reminders; the last reading already stored is not deleted by revoking the permission: it is kept until you sign out, delete your account, or an administrator disables or deletes it (see Section 6), and administrators can see it on the staff map — labelled with how long ago it was taken — for as long as it is stored and location sharing is turned on. The Lock Screen job card can be turned off in Settings → Notifications, and the home-screen widget by removing it from your Home Screen.
  • If you are a client of a business that uses the App, please direct access, correction, and deletion requests to that business, which controls its records; you may also contact us and we will assist or refer your request as appropriate.

We will respond to verified requests within the time required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
